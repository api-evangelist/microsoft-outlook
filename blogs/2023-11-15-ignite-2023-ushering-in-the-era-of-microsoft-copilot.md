Welcome At Ignite 2023 Microsoft Copilot is grabbing all the headlines. So of course, whether or not you’re a developer in the Microsoft 365 developer community, we’re very excited to announce that organizations with Microsoft 365 can now opt-in to the public preview of plugin extensibility! This means that developers can start building, testing, and publishing plugins that are available for administrators to discover and manage, and for end-users to use.
